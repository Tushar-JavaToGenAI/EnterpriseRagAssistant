# Enterprise Leave and Time-Off Policy

All regular full-time employees are eligible for paid time off benefits starting from their first day of employment. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for planned absences.

| Leave Type | Annual Allocation | Carry Forward Limit | Approval Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Casual Leave | 12 Days | 0 Days (Expires Dec 31) | Direct Manager |
| Sick Leave | 10 Days | 5 Days | Direct Manager |
| Earned / Privilege Leave | 18 Days | 15 Days | Department Head |
| Parental Leave | 12 Weeks | 0 Days | HR & Department Head |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 Days | 0 Days | Direct Manager |

### Special Leave Provisions

Employees experiencing unexpected medical emergencies exceeding three consecutive days must provide a certified medical practitioner certificate upon returning to work. Unused Sick Leave above the carry-forward limit cannot be encashed at the end of the calendar year.
